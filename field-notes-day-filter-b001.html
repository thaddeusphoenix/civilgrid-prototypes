Field Notes — Day Filter
Purpose

A water-utility customer asked to filter field-note photos by the day they were taken, then look at only that day's points on the map. This prototype compares two places the filter could live: in the Field Notes tab, or on the Field Notes layer in the map's layer picker.

Linear project

Field Notes viewing enhancements ↗

What to notice
  • A · In the tab: day chips sit above the photo list. Picking one or more days filters the list, the map pins and direction cones, and the export. "Select all" and "Export Images" act only on what's showing, with counts. The tab's list and its map already read the same data, so the map follows the filter with little extra work.
  • B · On the map layer: the same day filter is attached to the Field Notes layer in the layer picker, so it holds on the project map and any tab. This matches "filter, then look at the filtered layer on the map" more literally. It adds no list and no export.
  • Both show a filter pill at the top of the map so a filtered map is never mistaken for the full record. Its Clear button resets the filter.
  • Days are chips with counts, not a calendar. Capture days are few and scattered, and the tab already groups photos by day.
  • The photographer filter is shown disabled. It's the sibling request in the same project.
